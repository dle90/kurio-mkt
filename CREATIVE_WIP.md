## The ROAS rules every concept must obey (so you have them without the memory files)
Primary KPI is **ROAS** (order-based revenue, Getfly `ads_code` attribution) — **not** CPR.
Cheap CPR + low ROAS = junk leads. Spend-weighted present-vs-absent ROAS lift (~45 codes; directional, not causal):

- **USE (positive):** Transformation arc with a NUMBER — #1 lever (1.35×), should LEAD · EOY report-card moment (1.28×) · anti-học-thêm / anti-trung-tâm (1.24×) · AI-tutor "gợi mở tư duy, lộ trình cá nhân hóa" (1.18×) · tasteful emoji (1.27×).
- **NEUTRAL (floor, don't lead):** "3K/ngày" price anchor (1.02×) · exam mention · "hè/summer" framing.
- **AVOID (negative):** Scarcity/urgency — WORST (0.59×): no "suất", "nhanh tay", "giảm X% hôm nay", countdowns · pain/fear as the opening (0.70×) · dad narrator (0.64×) · teacher-voice narrator (0.83×). Narrate as a MOTHER.
- **TEST (one-week wobble):** ALL-CAPS headline flipped 1.24→0.77 — offer a sentence-case variant.
- **Format:** VIDEO best for scale (0.87 ROAS); dynamic-image as cheap A/B partner.
- **Landing page:** `gioitoan.kurio.vn/dang-ky` (consultation/telesales funnel — best ROAS 0.98 vs `/kurio` pricing page 0.51). The `-reup`/`-xpage` "LP families" were just utm tracking labels, NOT separate pages; "reup" = re-uploading a fatigued winner on a fresh code.

Current live winners to refresh/clone (05-31): `code13-reup` 1.60 · `cx` 1.41 · `intro2-xpage` 1.28 · `code45-reup` 1.04. Refresh cadence every 6–8 weeks (creative fatigue: a top ad's CPR ~doubles by month 3).

## TRACK B — modern / western (the exploration)

### Decisions locked this session
- **Aggressiveness: FULL WESTERN REBUILD.** Brand voice (not mom POV), short copy, product/abstract proof, sleek visual system — true Duolingo/Brilliant register. (Chosen over the lower-risk "visual-only swap".)
- **Audience: UNCHANGED — primary-school parents (mom of a lớp 1–5 kid).** Not shifting to teens. This is the *hardest* test — it's exactly where the quê formula already wins.
- **Assets: FULL KIT available** — Kuriomon character art, brand colors/type/style guide, polished app-UI screens. So the character-led and poster concepts are cheap to produce.

### The honest risk
A full rebuild drops THREE proven levers at once (mother narrator, long narrative, personal testimonial) — exactly the things the data says win for this audience. So this is a deliberate bet *against* current evidence: run it small, time-boxed, with a hard kill line — **not** a portfolio shift. If it loses we won't know *which* of the three flips caused it (the price of "full rebuild"; a round 2 would isolate one variable at a time).
Encouraging comp: **Elsa Speak** (VN-founded ed-app) proves a western aesthetic *can* work in VN ed-tech.

### Strategic reframe (because the buyer is a primary mom, not a teen)
A mom of a 7-year-old does NOT buy on "get smarter" identity. She buys on: *will my kid actually improve · will they stop fighting me about studying · is it legit/safe · is it worth the money.* Western concepts must dress THOSE criteria in modern clothing.

### Concept ranking
- ❌ **W1 — Brilliant-style abstract "thinking is beautiful" animation. CUT.** Sells the learner's self-image (irrelevant to a primary mom) and is the most expensive to make.
- ✅ **W2 — Kuriomon × AI-tutor app demo. LEAD.**
- ✅ **W3 — "6→9" minimalist poster system. LEAD.**
- 🤔 **W4 — Anti-học-thêm manifesto. Round 2.**

#### W2 — Kuriomon × AI tutor (character-led app demo)
Format: sleek vertical video / carousel. App screens in clean device frames; **Kuriomon** mascot animated reacting as the kid solves; motion-graphic captions; brand color system; playful-smart BRAND voice (no mom POV). Fully buildable from the existing kit.
Why it fits a primary mom: "my kid will actually *want* to use this / con tự giác" is a real mom desire — shown, not told, via the character + gamification + the genuinely differentiated *gợi mở* AI tutor.
- Headline A: *"Không quát, không ép — con tự mở Kurio học mỗi ngày."*
- Headline B: *"Gia sư AI 1:1 cho con. Con học mà cứ tưởng đang chơi."*
- Sub: *"AI gợi mở từng bước · Lộ trình riêng · Bám sát SGK."*  · CTA: *"Đăng ký học thử →"*
- Optional proof chip overlaid on the app screen: *"6→9 điểm"* / *"9.000+ bài"*.

#### W3 — "6→9" poster system (minimalist static)
Format: 4 minimalist posters, one grid — huge type + thin app screenshot + whitespace + Kurio logo. Cheapest, ships from the style guide now. Keeps the ONE lever preservable in full-western: a transformation NUMBER.
- *"6 → 9. Toán của con, sau một học kỳ với gia sư AI Kurio."*
- *"15 phút/ngày. Không trung tâm, không đưa đón."* (anti-học-thêm, clean)
- *"1 kèm 1. Gia sư AI gợi mở tư duy — không đọc đáp án cho chép."*
- 4th (value/curriculum): *"Bám sát SGK · Đối tác học thuật IKMC."*
- CTA strip: *"Học thử · gioitoan.kurio.vn"*

#### W4 — Anti-học-thêm manifesto (round 2)
Bold-type editorial: *"Con bạn không cần học thêm. Con bạn cần hiểu."* Looks 100% western, but secretly keeps the proven anti-học-thêm lever (1.24×). Hold until W2/W3 show signal.

### Funnel
Keep `/dang-ky` (consultation/telesales). For a primary-mom buyer the high-touch consult *fits* — moms of young kids often *want* to talk to a human before paying — and it's the best-ROAS funnel. The frictionless self-serve expectation is a teen/adult thing. **Guardrail:** the western CTA must stay honest — *"Đăng ký học thử" / "Nhận tư vấn"*, never a "play now" promise the consultation form then breaks.

### Test design (when we build)
- Run **W2 + W3 concurrently against B1.0 (the quê winner) as the live control** — same `/dang-ky` LP, same `complete_registration` optimization, same offer. Only the creative package differs.
- Budget: small, exploratory (~400–600k VND/day each); 14-day read.
- Targeting: same broad primary-mom (audience unchanged); optional **urban-skew A/B** (HCMC / HN tier-1, IG-forward) to give the look its best shot.
- **Read BOTH layers, not just win/lose:**
  - ROAS vs control → viability.
  - CTR / hook-rate / CPR vs control → does the look attract attention even if it doesn't convert?
  - Diagnostic: *"great CTR + weak ROAS"* = looks good, doesn't sell to moms; *"matches on both"* = aesthetic viable, expand.
- Decision tree (ROAS-primary, mirrors the B1.x briefs):
  - **SCALE:** ROAS ≥ control (~0.9) at ≥30 reg / ≥4M VND spend.
  - **ITERATE:** ROAS 0.5–0.9 with strong CTR/hook → fix message/funnel, keep the look.
  - **KILL:** ROAS <0.5 at sample; or junk-lead guard (cheap CPR + ROAS <0.4).
