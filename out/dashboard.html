2 · The lag — why daily ROAS lies

A registration becomes revenue days later. Revenue booked today reflects ad spend from the past, not today's.

Registration → first order lag
same day 480

n=1399 converted customers · median 1d · p75 9d · p90 44d
What this means
  • 61% of conversions land within 3 days, 73% within 7.
  • A registration cohort is only ~complete after 44 days (p90). Don't judge a code's ROAS before then.
  • Today's revenue line is still maturing — recent cohorts will keep adding orders for ~44 days.

Method. Daily spend + registrations from Meta campaign insights (60d). Revenue = approved sale_orders.real_amount.
