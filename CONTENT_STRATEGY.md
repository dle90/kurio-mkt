## 1. Strategy framework

### The premise

Three findings dictate everything below:

- **Creative fatigue is the dominant CPR driver.** Top ads' CPR ~doubles by month 3 of their lifetime. Ad-set age is irrelevant (r=0.071). Refresh cadence is the biggest controllable lever.
- **Targeting is marginal at scale** (47% top vs 63% bottom run interest stacks, p≈0.21). Don't over-engineer audiences; engineer creative supply.
- **The winning 7-trait formula is real but saturating.** All current top 15 ads are variants of the same testimonial template. The refresh queue must be net-new *angles*, not new names on the same story.

### The three jobs of the creative engine

1. **Hold ground** — keep 1–2 proven winners always-on as a control / floor on volume.
2. **Test angles** — ship 4–6 new angle hypotheses per phase (each tests *one* variable, not five).
3. **Refresh aggressively** — retire any creative at week 8 of its life, even if still positive. They spike CPR before going negative.

### Testing principles

- **One variable per test.** Hold targeting + LP + price anchor + format constant; vary only the angle (the headline + body framing). Otherwise you can't attribute the CPR delta.
- **Measurement window: 14 days minimum.** Daily CPR is too noisy (only ~35 reg/day combined across both accounts). Daily watch is for leading indicators only (CTR, hook rate, CPL).
- **Min sample to call a winner:** ≥30 reg or ≥4M VND spend per cell, whichever first. Below that, signal is unreliable.
- **Kill rule:** if CPR > 1.5× baseline (~420k VND) after 7 days with ≥10 reg → pause.
- **Scale rule:** if CPR < 0.7× baseline (~195k VND) after 7 days with ≥15 reg → 2× the daily budget.
- **Always run on `_xpage_kv` LP family** — −43% CPR vs alternatives. Non-`_xpage` LPs need explicit justification.

### The portfolio shape (steady state)

```
Always-on control (1–2 ads)         15% of spend  — proven floor
Angle tests in flight (4–6 ads)     50% of spend  — the refresh engine
Scale-ups (1–3 graduated winners)   30% of spend  — last cycle's winners getting more budget
Burn-down (1–2 retiring proven ads)  5% of spend  — declining tail of last cycle
```

Total: 7–13 active creatives at any time. Below 7 = under-testing; above 13 = under-statistical-power per cell.

## 2. Daily / weekly operating rhythm

### Daily (5 min, leading indicators only)

For each active ad cell, watch:

| Metric | Healthy | Yellow flag | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (link) | ≥0.7% | 0.4–0.7% | <0.4% |
| Video hook rate (25%) | ≥40% | 25–40% | <25% |
| CPLPV | ≤25k VND | 25–40k | >40k |
| CPL (link click) | ≤15k VND | 15–25k | >25k |

**Rule:** don't act on CPR within first 7 days. Only act on leading indicators if all four go red simultaneously.

### Weekly (Monday review, 30 min)

1. Pull last-7d ad-level performance for active cells.
2. Apply kill / scale rules per cell.
3. Decide: ship next week's batch from queue, or extend current cells if test windows haven't closed.
4. Update angle scoreboard (which Tier 1 blind spot has been validated / falsified).

### Bi-weekly (Mondays, every 2 weeks, 60 min)

1. **Cell verdict day.** Every cell that's hit its 14-day window or 30-reg / 4M-VND threshold gets a verdict: WINNER / NEUTRAL / LOSER.
2. **Promote winners** to scale-up budget tier.
3. **Retire week-6+ creatives** even if still positive (per fatigue rule).
4. **Brief next batch** (4–6 new cells) using angle queue.

### Monthly (last Friday, 90 min)

- Update CONTENT_STRATEGY.md with what worked / what didn't.
- Re-rank the angle queue based on new data.
- Re-baseline CPR (does it still match 278k? if drifting, recalibrate kill/scale thresholds).

### D. Format-mix decision (revised 2026-05-08)

Original plan assumed video-heavy production. Format-CPR analysis on the 228-ad dataset showed:

- Dynamic-image **210k VND CPR** at ≥3M spend; video **269k VND** at the same spend cut.
- Dynamic-image is 13% of inventory but **33% of the top-15 winners** — 2.5× overrepresented at the top.
- Video is 55% of inventory but only 33% of top-15 — meaningfully underrepresented.

**Revised production target:**
- **2 net-new videos / month** (lead-angle slots only — screen-addiction reframe and dad-voice testimonial need motion).
- **4–6 dynamic-image / month** (everything else).
- Estimated production cost: ~25–30M VND/month total (was ~40M+ with all-video).

If Kurio can hit 2 videos + 4–6 dynamic-images per month, this plan executes as-is. If not, drop video to 1/month for the screen-addiction lead and convert dad-voice to a dynamic-image with a high-quality dad portrait + long-body testimonial.

## 5. What this plan is NOT

- It's not a guarantee of CPR improvement. Plan it as 4–6 angle bets/month with maybe 1–2 winners. Most cells will be neutral; the wins compound.
- It's not a replacement for the team's editorial judgment. I produce structure + copy patterns; native-Vietnamese marketers tone-edit before launch.
- It's not exhaustive of the angle queue. Tier 2 / Tier 3 blind spots from `creative_blind_spots.md` (memory) stay in reserve for Phase C onward.
- It does not account for Tết (late Jan / early Feb) which is a near-dead window — flagged for next year's plan.
